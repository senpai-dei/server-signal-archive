# BIMI Mark Certificates (VMC & CMC) for Inbox Brand Visibility & Trust
**Link:** [Original Thread](https://lowendtalk.com/discussion/220123/bimi-mark-certificates-vmc-cmc-for-inbox-brand-visibility-trust)

Enhance your brand’s visibility and credibility in customers’ inboxes with [BIMI Mark Certificates](https://certera.com/mark-certificates "BIMI Mark Certificates"), including Verified Mark Certificates (VMC) and Common Mark Certificates (CMC). These certificates help organizations display their authenticated brand logo alongside email messages, giving recipients an immediate visual signal of legitimacy and strengthening brand recognition.

**Key Benefits:**  
**Brand Logo in the Inbox**: Display your verified brand logo with supported email providers.  
Build Customer Trust: Give recipients a clearer visual indication that emails are genuinely from your organization.  
**Combat Phishing & Spoofing:** BIMI works alongside email authentication standards such as SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.  
**Improve Brand Recognition:** Make legitimate communications easier to identify at a glance.  
**Support Email Security & Compliance:** Strengthen your organization’s overall email authentication and security strategy.  
**VMC & CMC Options:** Choose the certificate type appropriate for your brand, trademark status, and BIMI requirements.

[**Buy Verified Mark Certificate (VMC**)](https://certera.com/mark-certificates/digicert-verified-mark-certificate "Buy Verified Mark Certificate (VMC)") - Trademark Required  
Designed for organizations whose logos meet the applicable trademark and verification requirements. A VMC provides a high level of assurance that the displayed brand logo is associated with the authenticated organization.

[**Buy Common Mark Certificate (CMC)**](https://certera.com/mark-certificates/digicert-common-mark-certificate "Buy Common Mark Certificate (CMC)") - No Trademark Required
