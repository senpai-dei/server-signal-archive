3. How billing works,

All prices are in USD.  
We use hourly pricing, with compute charges prorated by actual billable seconds. The $4.99 and $9.99 prices are 730-hour reference amounts, not a requirement to prepay for a fixed monthly, quarterly or annual term.

Add funds to your account and use your balance for service charges.  
Powering off an instance does not stop compute billing, because its resources remain allocated.  
Successful deletion stops further compute billing for that instance.

Previously incurred usage charges remain payable.  
Billing guide: <https://koanode.com/docs/billing>

4. Traffic and networking

Outbound transfer is accounted for through a monthly regional Traffic Pool, shared by your instances within the same region. Traffic pools are separate between regions.

Inbound traffic is free.  
Regional overage rates apply if you exceed the included outbound pool.

The advertised 1 Gbps port speed is not a guarantee of sustained throughput to every destination. Actual performance depends on routing, destination networks and network conditions.
