### What's New?

**Private Networking**  
Create private LANs between your LayerOne VPS servers, allowing them to communicate without traversing the public network.

**Per-VM Firewalls**  
Create and manage firewall rules for individual VPS servers directly from the LayerOne panel.

**Floating IPs**  
Create floating IP addresses and move them between your servers whenever needed. Floating IPs are billed hourly and can be removed at any time.

**PTR / rDNS Management**  
PTR records can now be configured directly from the Network tab in the LayerOne panel.

**New Usage Graphs**  
Keep track of your server's resource and bandwidth usage from one convenient screen.

**Shared Bandwidth Pool**  
Your account starts with **2,000GB of included bandwidth**. Each additional VPS you deploy adds another **500GB** to your shared bandwidth pool.

**2.5Gbps Networking**  
All plans now have 2.5Gbps networking by default, as well as new 10Gbps network optimized plans
