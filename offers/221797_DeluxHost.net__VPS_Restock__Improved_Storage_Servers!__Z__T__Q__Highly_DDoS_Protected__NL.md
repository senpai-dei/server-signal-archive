🛡️ NETWORK & DDoS PROTECTION
============================

One of the biggest improvements to our infrastructure over the past months has been the continued use of **PletX DDoS protection** across the network.

After the network changes and mitigation work carried out earlier this year, PletX has now been protecting our infrastructure for several months and has become a stable part of the DeluxHost network.

The current setup has significantly improved our ability to absorb and handle attacks and abnormal traffic events compared with the situation before these changes.

Of course, no DDoS mitigation solution can guarantee that every possible attack will have absolutely zero impact, but overall the network is now operating from a considerably stronger and more stable foundation.

We continue to monitor routing, attack patterns and upstream behaviour and will keep making improvements where necessary.

All VPS infrastructure included in this thread is protected by **PletX**.
