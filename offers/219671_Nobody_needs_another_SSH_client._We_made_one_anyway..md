# Nobody needs another SSH client. We made one anyway.
**Link:** [Original Thread](https://lowendtalk.com/discussion/219671/nobody-needs-another-ssh-client-we-made-one-anyway)

![](https://asganaflow.s-ul.eu/cB5if9Hs)

It's called CloudTerm. Free, no account, no subscription, no "contact sales" button.

[![GitHub Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Star%20on%20GitHub-181717?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/BradPerbs/cloudterm)

(We're CloudBlast, we do VPS hosting. This isn't an offer, we're not selling  
anything, we just built a thing and figured this crowd would actually use it.)

Basically it's SSH + SFTP + RDP + VNC + serial + port forwarding in one window,  
so you can stop running PuTTY, WinSCP and mstsc side by side like it's 2009.

What's in it:

* SSH, telnet and serial, tabs and split panes
* SFTP over the connection you already opened, drag and drop
* RDP and VNC in a pane
* Port forwards, local, remote and SOCKS5, with live traffic counters
* Jump hosts, proxies, 2FA, auto reconnect when your laptop wakes up
* Encrypted vault for keys and passwords
* Imports your `~/.ssh/config`, or your old PuTTY, KiTTY and MobaXterm sessions + Exports Available

Genuinely useful for organising the 40 idle VPSes you swear you're going to use.

---

**AI Lovers**  
There's an optional AI panel too. It runs on the **Claude Code** or **Codex** login you  
already have, so we're not reselling you tokens or hoovering up your sessions.  
Hate AI? Ignore it, nothing else breaks.

---

**Free cloud sync, and we actually mean free.** Your hosts, folders, keys,  
snippets and settings are encrypted on your machine and synced to a CloudBlast  
account, so a new laptop is a login instead of an evening of copying things  
around. You don't need to be a customer. You don't need a single active server  
with us. There's no paid tier of it waiting to upsell you later. It's also  
completely optional, the app works offline and won't nag you about it.

This is something we offer as Hosting Provider to promote the use of the terminal.
