# 10 Gbps port now available on CubePath VPS
**Link:** [Original Thread](https://lowendtalk.com/discussion/223037/10-gbps-port-now-available-on-cubepath-vps)

Hi all,

You can now run a CubePath VPS on a **10 Gbps public port** instead of the standard 1 Gbps. It's an add-on you tick when you deploy.

**Price:** $20/month, billed by the hour like the VPS itself ($0.0274/h), with no setup fee.

**Available in:** Barcelona, Houston and Miami, on any VPS plan in a project billed by the hour.

**How to get it:**

* **New VPS:** when you deploy, open Advanced Settings and tick "10 Gbps port" under Addons.

**The fine print:**

* Your plan's traffic allowance doesn't change. Traffic beyond it is billed at $2 per TB, as on any of our VPS, and the port stays at 10 Gbps. At full speed, a 10 TB plan uses its allowance in under three hours.

Full details: <https://cubepath.com/changelog/vps-10-gbps-port>

Happy to answer any questions here.
