PROMO RULES

* One promotional instance per customer per campaign per Region.
* The three promotions are independent from each other.
* Each customer may use each promotion once in Miami, once in Frankfurt and once in Amsterdam, subject to eligibility and availability.
* Promotional instances do not consume the standard 10-instance account quota.
* Quantity is limited to one instance when a promo code is applied.
* Promotional eligibility is not restored after deleting an instance.
* Promotional resources and prices are recorded in the billing snapshot at creation.
