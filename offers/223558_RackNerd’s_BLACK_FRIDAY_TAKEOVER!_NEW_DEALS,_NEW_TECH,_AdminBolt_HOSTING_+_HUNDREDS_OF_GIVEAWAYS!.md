LET’S TAKE A CLOSER LOOK AT WHAT’S NEW!
=======================================

[![](https://blog.racknerd.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ctrl-features.png)](https://www.racknerd.com/ctrl-control-panel)

**ctrl. Built by RackNerd, for RackNerd VPS customers!**

Earlier this year, we introduced **ctrl**, our proprietary VPS control panel designed to make managing your RackNerd VPS easier and more convenient.

From ctrl, you can reboot your VPS, reinstall or change your operating system, open an HTML5 console, reset your root password, monitor usage, and more.

Want to get an application up and running? ctrl’s **One-Click Apps** make it easy to get started with tools such as **OpenClaw, Hermes Agent, AnythingLLM, Ollama, LibreChat, n8n, AdminBolt, Coolify, Portainer, WireGuard, and more!**

We’ve also introduced the **ctrl Network Firewall**, an optional layer of protection that filters inbound traffic on our network before it reaches your VPS. You can enable it and manage your rules directly from ctrl!

**ctrl, One-Click Apps, and the ctrl Network Firewall are included FREE with your RackNerd VPS** -- and existing customers get these features too! You also retain full root access, giving you the freedom to install and configure your own software at any time.

Choose a VPS with enough resources for the application you plan to run. Please note that installing a One-Click App reinstalls the VPS and erases its existing data, so back up anything important first.

Learn more about ctrl: <https://www.racknerd.com/ctrl-control-panel>
